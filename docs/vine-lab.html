Vine growth, four treatments

The same module the site runs, instantiated four times with the same seed. Watch the tips rather than the shape — raw, creep and pop grow identical plants and differ only in motion. Press replay to see them start together.

560px 1.00×

One 30fps loop drives all four, matching the cap the real page runs at. “Frames that moved” is the share of painted frames whose pixels differ from the frame before — higher is smoother. Typical readings once they are growing: raw ~29%, creep ~82%, pop ~99%, fine ~58%.

raw sits low because growth steps at about half the paint rate, so most frames are identical to the one before — that is the stutter. creep does not reach 100%, and the missing 18% is not a shortfall: everything is snapped to a 3px pixel-art grid, so a frame where the tip advances less than one block draws exactly the same image. pop is near 100% because an alpha ramp on the newest segments changes something every frame whether or not the tip moved. fine gains by stepping twice as often, which is also why it has 2.7× the segments and is a different plant.

Worth saying: this is not a performance problem. The vine’s whole tick — grow and paint — measures 0.29ms on the real page, which is 0.9% of a 33ms frame.
